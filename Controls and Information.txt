The controls and other information for "The Steel Hearted".

What is "The Steel Hearted"?
The Steel Hearted is a turn-based mech. Players can explore
a select few star systems, and fight enemies in order to 
eventually their mech for greater combat prowess. Mechs come
with 'support cores', tools that alter a mech's move set, and
which allow for more varied combat.

Menu controls:
- Left click to interact with menus

Space controls:
- move ship with WASD
- change systems with 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 (along top of keyboard)
- press E when icon appears to trade with capital, or fight base
- press P to pause the game, can save and quit here

Battle controls:
- 1, melee attack
- 2, guard
- 3, laser attack
- 4, missile attack
- hold down 5 + press numpad 1 to 8, switch cores

Core Switch (in battle while holding down 5):
- Numpad 1, base core, low armor damage with some health damage
- Numpad 2, base core, medium armor damage with little health damage
- Numpad 3, base core, heavy armor damage with one health damage
- Numpad 4, purchased core, gain charge and deal little armor damage
- Numpad 5, purchased core, decrease missile ammo cost by small amount
- Numpad 6, purchased core, decrease missile ammo cost by large amount
- Numpad 7, purchased core, decrease damage and charge cost of laser
- Numpad 8, purchased core, increase damage and charge cost of laser

Game Objectives:
- Defeat all bases in the United Nations of Earth
- Defeat all bases in the Grand Revolutionaries
- Defeat all bases in the Imperium
- Defeat all bases in the Pristine Collective
- Defeat the base in the Golden Empire, available once all other bases have been defeated

Defeating a base rewards you with some currency, which can be used
to purchase mech frame upgrades or support cores at a system's capital planet.

Frame Upgrades increase your mech's armor and damage.
Support Cores increase your health, and offers move variations (see above).

You can fight already defeated bases again for lesser rewards.
